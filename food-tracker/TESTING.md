# Phone test checklist

Plain steps. If anything looks wrong, take a screenshot and send it to Andrew.

Type the address exactly as **food.pivotaiglobal.com**, with nothing after "com".

## New layout: three tabs and a big mic

1. Open the app. You should land on **Overview**, with three tabs along the bottom: **Overview**, **Diary** and **Goals**, and a big green **mic** just above them in the middle. The app is always light, even if your phone is in dark mode.
2. The top should greet you by name ("Good morning, ..."). Once you've logged two days in a row, a small 🌱 count shows under the date.
3. On **Today**, a big ring shows calories, and three smaller rings show protein (mint), carbs (apricot) and fat (lavender). Past a goal, a ring closes in a soft tan, never red.
4. Tap **Diary**. You should see today's total, the food box, and what you've eaten so far, each food with a little picture (🥚 eggs, 🍌 banana, 🍺 beer). Foods it has no picture for get a plate.
5. Tap the back arrow to see yesterday. Tap **Diary** at the bottom again: it goes back to today.
6. Log something for breakfast, lunch and dinner on the same day. When the third one goes in, a little confetti burst should pop once.
7. Tap **Goals**. Change your calorie goal and tap **Save changes**. It should say **Saved.** Tap **Overview**: the new goal shows there.
8. Tap in the food box on Diary. While the keyboard is up, the tabs and mic hide so you have room to type.
9. Add a food. The **Check and add** screen has no tabs; after **Add** you're back on Diary.

10. Add it to your home screen: in Safari tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The icon should be a green tile with a little sprout inside a mint, apricot and lavender ring. If you added it before, delete the old one first so the new icon shows.

## Voice and drinks

1. Tap the big green **mic** above the tabs. It works from any tab: it jumps to **Diary** and starts listening.
2. The first time, your phone asks to use the microphone. Tap **Allow**.
3. The mic pulses. Say **two eggs and a banana for breakfast**. Your words should show up in the box as you talk.
4. Stop talking. After a second the **Check and add** screen should open with eggs and a banana, and **Breakfast** picked. Nothing is saved until you tap **Add**.
5. Tap the mic and say nothing. It should say it didn't catch that.
6. Tap the mic, start talking, then tap it again. It should stop and use what it heard.
7. Say a few drinks: **a Blue Moon**, **two Bud Lights**, **a pint of Michelob Ultra**, **a glass of wine**, **a White Claw**. Each should come up as a beer, wine or seltzer with calories, not soda or something random.
8. Typing still works the same as before.

If the mic button is missing, your browser doesn't support voice. Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. If you tapped **Don't Allow** by mistake on iPhone: tap **aA** in the address bar, then **Website Settings**, then set **Microphone** to **Allow**.

- Did any drink match something weird?

## Dashboard and weight

1. Open the app on **Overview**. Under the date you should see **Today | Week | Month**.
2. Tap **Week**. You should see your average calories a day, how many days you logged, and (if weight tracking is on) your weight trend, then a chart with a colored bar for each day.
3. Each bar is split into protein (mint), carbs (apricot) and fat (lavender). The gray line is your goal.
4. Tap any bar. The box above the chart should switch to that day's numbers. Tap **Open this day** to see what you ate that day.
5. Tap **Month**. You should see 30 days. Close the app and open it again: it should still be on Month.
6. Tap **Show as table**. You should see the same numbers as a list.
7. If **Track my weight** is on in **Goals**: type your weight in **Today's weight** and tap **Save**. On Week or Month, a pink line (your 7-day average) and pink dots (each weigh-in) should show on the right-hand scale.
8. Save a different weight the same day. It should replace the first one, not add a second.

9. Optional: on **Goals**, fill in **Calories you burn a day** (your baseline, from a fitness watch, your doctor or an online calculator) and save. On **Week** and **Month** a dashed line shows it next to the solid goal line, and the sentence above the chart says how your average compares. On **Today**, under the big ring, it says how far over or under it you are.

## Phase 3: Log food by typing

1. Open **https://food.pivotaiglobal.com** and tap **Diary**. You should see **Today** with your calorie goal and a box that says **What did you eat?**
2. Type **two eggs and a slice of toast** and tap **Add**. A **Check and add** screen should show two cards: eggs and toast, each with an amount and calories.
3. Check the meal buttons at the top. Pick the right one.
4. On the toast card, change the amount to 2. The calories should update right away.
5. Tap **Add 2 foods**. You should land back on Diary with both foods listed and the calorie total going up.
6. Tap the eggs row. Change it to 3 and tap **Save**. The calories should go up.
7. Tap the toast row, tap **Remove**, then **Yes, remove**. It should disappear and the total should drop.
8. Try a meal word: **a banana for a snack**. The confirm screen should already have **Snack** picked.
9. Try something made up, like **a zorblax bar**. It should say it couldn't find it. Tap **Enter it yourself**, type 200 for calories, and add it.
10. Try a few real things you actually ate today. For each one, check: is it the right food? Is the amount about right? If a match looks wrong, tap **Change** and pick a better one.
11. Tap the **back arrow** at the top to go to yesterday, then come back to today.
12. Try typing **I had six ounces of chicken breast and a cup of rice for dinner**. Both should show up, under Dinner.

## Phase 2: Set up your profile

1. Open **https://food.pivotaiglobal.com** and sign in with your email and code.
2. You should see **Welcome! Let's get you set up.**
3. Tap **Save and start** without filling anything in. Short notes should tell you what's missing. Nothing should save.
4. Type your name. The note under it should disappear.
5. Check the time zone. It should already show where you live.
6. Type a daily calorie goal, like 2000. Try 1000 first: a gentle note should suggest checking with a doctor. It should still let you save.
7. Fill in protein, carbs or fat if you want. Leaving them empty is fine.
8. Leave **Track my weight** off, or turn it on and enter your weight. Your goal weight is optional.
9. Tap **Save and start**. You should land on a page that says **Hi,** and your name, with your goals.
10. Tap **Goals** at the bottom. Change your calorie goal and tap **Save changes**. **Overview** should show the new number.
11. Close the browser completely and open the link again. Your name and goals should still be there.
12. In **Goals**, scroll down and tap **Sign out**. Signing back in should bring back the same profile, not the setup screen.

## Phase 1: Can you get in?

Use your phone. Try it once on cellular and once on Wi-Fi if you can.

1. Open **https://food.pivotaiglobal.com** in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. You should see a Cloudflare page asking for your email. Type your email and tap the button.
3. Check your email for a 6-digit code. It comes from Cloudflare. Look in spam if it's not there in a minute.
4. Type the code on the phone. You should land on a page that says **Hello,** and your email.
5. Under that, you should see two green checks: **Sign-in works** and **Database connected**.
6. Close the browser tab completely, then open the link again. You should go straight in, no code this time.
7. Tap **Sign out**. Open the link again. It should ask for your email again.
8. Optional: in Safari, tap Share > **Add to Home Screen**. Open it from the icon. Same Hello page.
9. Optional: switch your phone to dark mode. The app stays light and easy to read.

### Things that should NOT work

10. Ask someone whose email is not on the list to open the link and try. They should not get a code that works, and should never see the Hello page.
