# Phone test checklist

Plain steps. If anything looks wrong, take a screenshot and send it to Andrew.

Type the address exactly as **food.pivotaiglobal.com**, with nothing after "com".

## New layout: three tabs and a big mic

1. Open the app. You should land on **Overview**, with three tabs along the bottom: **Overview**, **Diary** and **Goals**, and a big green **mic** just above them in the middle. The app is always light, even if your phone is in dark mode.
2. The top should greet you by name ("Good morning, ..."). Once you've logged two days in a row, a small 🌱 count shows under the date.
3. On **Today**, a big ring shows calories, split into protein (mint), carbs (apricot), fat (lavender) and other (gray) like the Week bars, with each one's share under it ("31% protein · 26% carbs ..."). It goes as far round as you are toward your goal and fills the whole ring once you're over; the middle says how many are left or over. Three smaller rings show protein, carbs and fat grams. Past a goal, a small ring closes in a soft tan, never red.
4. Tap **Diary**. At the top, a green **Today's food** header shows the date, the day's calories against your goal with a bar, the protein, carbs and fat, and how much is left. What you've eaten so far sits right under it by meal; scroll down and the green header stays pinned at the top until the meals end. Below the meals come the food box (type, mic, barcode), then your usual foods. Each food has a little picture (🥚 eggs, 🍌 banana, 🍺 beer); foods it has no picture for get a plate. On other days the header says **Yesterday's food** or the weekday. Past your goal, the bar and the "over" number turn a warm apricot, never red.
5. Tap the back arrow to see yesterday. Tap **Diary** at the bottom again: it goes back to today.
6. Log something for breakfast, lunch and dinner on the same day. When the third one goes in, a little confetti burst should pop once.
7. Tap **Goals**. Change your calorie goal and tap **Save changes**. It should say **Saved.** Tap **Overview**: the new goal shows there.
8. Tap in the food box on Diary. While the keyboard is up, the tabs and mic hide so you have room to type.
9. Add a food. The **Check and add** screen has no tabs; after **Add** you're back on Diary.

10. Add it to your home screen: in Safari tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The icon should be a mint tile with a smiling white bowl holding a tomato, a blueberry and a leaf. If you added it before, delete the old one first so the new icon shows.

## Apple Watch

1. On **Goals**, scroll to **Apple Watch** and tap **Connect my Apple Watch**. It shows your sync key, the sync address, and the steps.
2. Follow the steps in the **Shortcuts** app on your iPhone. Use **Copy** to bring the key and address over. Name the shortcut **Food Sync** exactly.
3. Run it once. Allow it to read Health data and to connect. It should say "Synced today" with your active calories, resting calories and steps.
4. Open the food app. On **Overview**, **Today** shows "___ cal burned so far" and your steps under the ring. Compare them with the Fitness app: they should be close. If steps look about double, tell Andrew.
5. Tap **Sync now** on Today. It opens Shortcuts and runs it. Come back to the food app: the numbers update.
6. Tomorrow, look at **Week**: yesterday has a short dark tick for what your Watch says you burned, and tapping the day says how far under or over that you ate. Under the calorie chart, a small blue chart shows your steps each day, lined up with the days above. Tap a day in either chart and both follow.
7. On **Goals**, **Make a new key** and **Disconnect** each ask first. After either, the old Shortcut says "That sync key isn't right."

### Tell Andrew

- Did the Shortcut steps match what you saw on your phone? Which step was confusing?
- Did the numbers match the Fitness app?

## Scanning a package

1. On **Diary**, tap the barcode button next to **Add**. The first time, your phone asks to use the camera. Tap **Allow**.
2. Hold a package's barcode (the lines with numbers under them) inside the white box. It should buzz or close on its own within a second or two.
3. The **Check and add** screen shows the product by brand and name, at one serving from the label, with its calories. Check it against the label. Change the amount if you had more or less, then tap **Add**.
4. Try a few kinds: a cereal box, a can or bottle, a snack bar, something small like gum (its barcode is short).
5. If it says **We don't know this barcode yet**, search for it by name, or tap **Enter it yourself** and copy the numbers from the label.
6. Tap the barcode button again, then **Type the number instead**. Type the digits under the lines and tap **Look up**.
7. If you tapped **Don't Allow** for the camera: it goes straight to typing the number. On iPhone, tap **aA** in the address bar, then **Website Settings**, then set **Camera** to **Allow**.

### Tell Andrew

- Which products did it get wrong, or not know? Write down the name and the numbers under the barcode.
- Did the calories match the label?

## Your weight plan

1. On **Goals**, fill in **Calories you burn a day** if you haven't (sex, age, activity, weight).
2. Under it, **Your weight plan**: tap **Lose**, type **1**, and keep **a week**. It should say "Eat about ___ cal a day", that it's 500 under what you burn, and show protein, carbs and fat. With a goal weight set, it says about when you'd reach it.
3. Switch to **a month** and type **4**: about 460 a day under.
4. Type something too fast, like **3** a week: it keeps it to a safe pace and says so.
5. Tap **Use these as my daily goals**. The page jumps up to **Daily goals** with the new numbers filled in and saved. Check **Overview** and **Diary**: the new goal shows there.
6. Leave and come back to Goals: the plan card remembers what you picked.
7. Try **Keep** (eat what you burn) and **Gain**.

- Was anything confusing?

## Your usual foods

1. Log a few things over a couple of days the normal way (typing or the mic).
2. On **Diary**, under the food box, **Your usual foods** lists what you log most, each with the amount you had last time and its calories.
3. The meal buttons above the list start on the meal for this time of day. Foods you usually have at that meal come first. Tap **Dinner** and the order should change.
4. Tap a food. It turns into a green check, and it shows up in that meal below with the same amount as last time. The total at the top goes up.
5. A note says what was added, with **Undo**. Tap **Undo**: the food comes back out.
6. Tap two foods quickly, one after the other. Both should be added.
7. If you ate a different amount this time, tap the food in the meal list below and change it, like any other food.

## Restaurants

2. Try the other chains the same way, saying where it's from:
   - **8 nuggets and waffle fries from Chick-fil-A**
   - **a 6 inch Italian BMT from Subway**, then **a footlong turkey from Subway**
   - **a number 9 from Jimmy John's**

5. Plain words with no restaurant still work as before: **fries** or **a chicken sandwich** are generic.

## Voice and drinks

1. Tap the big green **mic** above the tabs. It works from any tab: it jumps to **Diary** and starts listening.
2. The first time, your phone asks to use the microphone. Tap **Allow**.
3. The mic pulses. Say **two eggs and a banana for breakfast**. Your words should show up in the box as you talk.
4. Stop talking. After a second the **Check and add** screen should open with eggs and a banana, and **Breakfast** picked. Nothing is saved until you tap **Add**.
5. Tap the mic and say nothing. It should say it didn't catch that.
6. Tap the mic, start talking, then tap it again. It should stop and use what it heard.
7. Say a few drinks: **a Blue Moon**, **two Bud Lights**, **a pint of Michelob Ultra**, **a glass of wine**, **a White Claw**. Each should come up as a beer, wine or seltzer with calories, not soda or something random.
8. Typing still works the same as before.

If the mic button is missing, your browser doesn't support voice. Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. If you tapped **Don't Allow** by mistake on iPhone: tap **aA** in the address bar, then **Website Settings**, then set **Microphone** to **Allow**.

## Dashboard and weight

1. Open the app on **Overview**. Under the date you should see **Today | Week | Month**. Scroll down: the greeting and those three buttons stay at the top while the cards slide under them.
2. Tap **Week**. The chart comes first, with a colored bar for each day. Under it: what the colors mean, the day you tapped, then your average calories a day, how many days you logged, and (if weight tracking is on) your weight trend.
3. Each bar is split into protein (mint), carbs (apricot) and fat (lavender), plus gray on top for calories the macros don't cover (a food typed in with calories only, the alcohol in a drink). The whole bar is the day's calories. The gray line is your goal.
4. Tap any bar. The box under the chart should switch to that day's numbers. Tap **Open this day** to see what you ate that day.
5. Tap **Month**. You should see 30 days. Close the app and open it again: it should still be on Month.
6. Tap **Show as table**. You should see the same numbers as a list.
7. If **Track my weight** is on in **Goals**: type your weight in **Today's weight** and tap **Save**. On Week or Month, a pink line (your 7-day average) and pink dots (each weigh-in) should show on the right-hand scale.
8. Save a different weight the same day. It should replace the first one, not add a second.

9. On **Goals**, under **Calories you burn a day**, pick your sex, type your age, pick how active you are, and (optional) your height. It should show "You burn about ___ cal a day" as you type. Save. On **Week** and **Month** a dashed line shows it next to the solid goal line, and the sentence above the chart says how your average compares. On **Today**, under the big ring, it says how far over or under it you are.

## Phase 2: Set up your profile

1. Open **https://food.pivotaiglobal.com** and sign in with your email and code.
2. You should see **Welcome! Let's get you set up.**
3. Tap **Save and start** without filling anything in. Short notes should tell you what's missing. Nothing should save.
4. Type your name. The note under it should disappear.
5. Check the time zone. It should already show where you live.
6. Type a daily calorie goal, like 2000. Try 1000 first: a gentle note should suggest checking with a doctor. It should still let you save.
7. Fill in protein, carbs or fat if you want. Leaving them empty is fine.
8. Leave **Track my weight** off, or turn it on and enter your weight. Your goal weight is optional.
9. Tap **Save and start**. You should land on a page that says **Hi,** and your name, with your goals.
10. Tap **Goals** at the bottom. Change your calorie goal and tap **Save changes**. **Overview** should show the new number.
11. Close the browser completely and open the link again. Your name and goals should still be there.
12. In **Goals**, scroll down and tap **Sign out**. Signing back in should bring back the same profile, not the setup screen.

## Phase 1: Can you get in?
